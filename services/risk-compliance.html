CAPABILITY

Risk Management & Compliance

Risk identification, risk registers, internal control, regulatory compliance and governance support.

This capability is framed around practical professional work: understanding the issue, examining the evidence, identifying material risks or gaps, and helping decision-makers establish a clearer basis for action.

Scope of capability

  • Enterprise and operational risk assessment
  • Risk registers and control mapping
  • Compliance monitoring
  • Governance and control frameworks

Why this matters

Risk management should be connected to strategy, operations, accountability and the controls that actually operate day to day.

Relevant experience

Documented experience includes ERM procedures, risk workshops, compliance monitoring and control frameworks.

PROFESSIONAL APPROACH

Evidence first. Judgement with context.

The objective is not to promise a predetermined outcome. It is to bring structured analysis, professional discipline and relevant experience to the question at hand.

For organisations operating in Nigeria, that means considering the regulatory, financial, operational and governance realities that shape how a recommendation can actually be implemented.
