Alumni

The client requested an Alumni area. The supplied résumé documents educational institutions but does not establish an alumni network, programme, statistics or testimonials.

Professional principles

Integrity is part of the professional architecture.

  • Integrity
    Act with honesty and professional responsibility.
  • Accountability
    Maintain clear responsibility for professional work and communication.
  • Competence
    Apply appropriate knowledge, analysis and professional judgement.
  • Confidentiality
    Treat professional information with appropriate care.
  • Respect & Fairness
    Engage people and institutions with dignity and fairness.
  • Compliance
    Respect applicable rules, policies and professional requirements.
Governance note

This page therefore serves as an academic identity/discovery space. It does not assert an alumni association or network.
