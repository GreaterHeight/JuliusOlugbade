General

About the platform

Who is Dr. Julius Olugbade?

Dr. Julius Olugbade is a professional and executive whose work spans accounting, audit, risk, compliance, financial management, advisory, leadership, professional education and business knowledge.

What is JuliusOlugbade.com?

JuliusOlugbade.com is Dr. Julius Olugbade's professional knowledge platform. It brings together his professional profile, capabilities, industry perspectives, discourse, research, education and training-related work.

What is Dr. Julius Olugbade's current executive role?

Dr. Julius Olugbade is the Managing Partner/CEO at Olusola Adekanola (OA) Group.

What does the website cover?

The platform covers professional services, industry experience, emerging issues and discourse, research and publications, education, training, career information and professional perspectives.

Professional Capabilities

Services and engagement

What professional services are presented on the platform?

The Services section covers Financial Analysis, Forecasting & Advisory; Audit & Internal Audit; Bank Compliance; Finance, IFRS & Financial Reporting; Fraud Prevention, Investigation & AML; Risk Management & Compliance; Tax Advisory & Compliance; and Leadership, Accounting & Professional Training.

Who can engage for professional services?

Organizations, businesses, institutions and professional stakeholders whose needs align with the capabilities presented on the Services and Industries sections can initiate a professional conversation through the Contact page.

How do I discuss a potential engagement?

Use the Contact page to provide your name, organization, subject and a concise description of the matter you would like to discuss. Direct contact details are also provided there.

Does the platform provide services across multiple industries?

Yes. The Industries section presents experience and professional context across banking and financial institutions, audit/tax/consulting, aviation and transport, construction and real estate, finance/leasing/logistics, pharmaceutical/healthcare, technology/telecommunications, and academia/professional education.
