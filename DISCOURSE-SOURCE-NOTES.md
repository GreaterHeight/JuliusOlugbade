# Discourse Source Notes

The discourse pages are written as professional educational/editorial content rather than as claims of client work or unpublished personal opinion.

Current Nigerian regulatory references used in the content should be checked against the primary regulator before publication updates:
- Financial Reporting Council of Nigeria (FRC): accounting standards, corporate reporting, sustainability reporting and audit-related guidance.
- Corporate Affairs Commission (CAC): company registration, corporate compliance and CAMA-related requirements.
- EFCC / SCUML: anti-money-laundering, counter-terrorist-financing and designated non-financial business/profession regulations.

The pages deliberately avoid inventing current statistics, client names, engagements, partnerships or professional achievements. Current economic, tax, regulatory and legal claims should be refreshed from primary Nigerian sources when the editorial pages are updated.
